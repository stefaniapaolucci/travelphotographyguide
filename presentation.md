## IDEA  

The main idea is to create a comprehensive <b><u>guide</b></u> for travel photography lovers and provide useful <b><u>resources</b></u> to improve photography skills, choose equipment, and connect with other enthusiasts.

---

## TOPIC

The website focuses on <b><u>travel photography</b></u>, combining practical tips and inspiration for explorers and photographers.

Travel photography captures the essence of <b><u>exploration</b></u> and <b><u>creativity</b></u>. It is a way to inspire others to see the world through a unique lens.

---

## WEBSITE STRUCTURE 

The website is organised into <b>4 main sections</b>, accessible through the menu at the top of the page:

----

- <b>HOME</b>: an introduction to the website and highlights of the main sections.

- <b>GUIDES</b>: a section dedicated to practical guides on how to photograph specific scenarios, such as The Northern Lights in Norway or The Cherry Blossom Season in Japan.

----

- <b>EQUIPMENT</b>: a section dedicated to advice and reviews on cameras, lenses, and accessories useful for travel.

- <b>COMMUNITY</b>: a section dedicated to a forum and events, ideal to share photos, join discussions, and find inspiration.

---

## TOOLS USED 

- <b>HTML</b>: used to create a clear and semantic structure for the website.

- <b>CSS</b>: defines the visual style, ensuring an intuitive and attractive navigation.

- <b>JAVASCRIPT</b>: adds interactivity and advanced features, such as cookies.
