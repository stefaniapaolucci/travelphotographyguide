Travel Photography Guide

Stefania Paolucci

2024/2025

IDEA

The main idea is to create a comprehensive guide for travel photography lovers and provide useful resources to improve photography skills, choose equipment, and connect with other enthusiasts.

TOPIC

The website focuses on travel photography, combining practical tips and inspiration for explorers and photographers.

Travel photography captures the essence of exploration and creativity. It is a way to inspire others to see the world through a unique lens.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE

The website is organised into 4 main sections, accessible through the menu at the top of the page:

  • HOME: an introduction to the website and highlights of the main sections.

  • GUIDES: a section dedicated to practical guides on how to photograph specific scenarios, such as The Northern Lights in Norway or The Cherry Blossom Season in Japan.

  • EQUIPMENT: a section dedicated to advice and reviews on cameras, lenses, and accessories useful for travel.

  • COMMUNITY: a section dedicated to a forum and events, ideal to share photos, join discussions, and find inspiration.

TOOLS USED

  • HTML: used to create a clear and semantic structure for the website.

  • CSS: defines the visual style, ensuring an intuitive and attractive navigation.

  • JAVASCRIPT: adds interactivity and advanced features, such as cookies.
